How to get a reliable signal
  • Plug the IR blaster firmly into the headphone jack.
  • Set media volume to 100% — the blaster is powered by the audio signal.
  • Turn Bluetooth audio off so sound routes to the jack.
  • Disable equalizers / sound effects (Dolby, etc.) — they distort the carrier.
  • Aim the blaster at the device from a few meters or less.
